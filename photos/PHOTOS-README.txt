HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
======================

Save each of the 9 photos you shared into this folder using EXACTLY these filenames:

GALLERY (in the order you want them displayed):
  gallery-1.jpg   → Photo 1
  gallery-2.jpg   → Photo 2  (this one spans 2 columns — best for landscape/wide shots)
  gallery-3.jpg   → Photo 3
  gallery-4.jpg   → Photo 4
  gallery-5.jpg   → Photo 5  (also spans 2 columns)
  gallery-6.jpg   → Photo 6
  gallery-7.jpg   → Photo 7
  gallery-8.jpg   → Photo 8
  gallery-9.jpg   → Photo 9

Suggested mapping from the photos you shared:
  gallery-1.jpg   → Outdoor campus — hugging side by side (first shot)
  gallery-2.jpg   → Outdoor campus — back-to-back, arms crossed (landscape feel)
  gallery-3.jpg   → Indoor — woman in red + man in navy, smiling at camera
  gallery-4.jpg   → Indoor — woman in red + man in navy, laughing together
  gallery-5.jpg   → Indoor — woman in red + man in navy, looking at each other
  gallery-6.jpg   → Indoor — woman in white seated, man in white kaftan standing
  gallery-7.jpg   → Outdoor campus — man looking at woman, her looking up at him
  gallery-8.jpg   → Indoor — one of the remaining hotel shots
  gallery-9.jpg   → Outdoor campus — another angle with trees

STORY SECTION (used in the "Our Story" section):
  story-1.jpg or story-2.jpg — NOT required if gallery-7.jpg and gallery-5.jpg exist.
  The site already uses gallery-7.jpg and gallery-5.jpg for the story section.

HERO BACKGROUND:
  hero-bg.jpg     → The full-screen background on the landing/hero section.
                    Best: a wide landscape or portrait photo of the two of you together.
                    Suggested: use the outdoor campus shot or indoor hotel shot.
                    Minimum recommended size: 1400px wide.

TIPS:
- JPG format recommended for photos (smaller file size, fast loading)
- Keep individual photos under 500 KB if possible (use TinyJPG or Squoosh to compress)
- All images are loaded with loading="lazy" so the page stays fast
- You can add more than 9 gallery photos — just add more .gallery-item divs in index.html
