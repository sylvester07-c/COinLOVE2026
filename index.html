How It All Began

Our Story

✦

Our journey is a testament to God's faithfulness, from whom all blessings flow. Years ago, we both attended membership class at Sovereign Grace Bible Church, Lagos and even belonged to the same prayer group. Though our paths were completely different then and we rarely spoke, God had a plan we couldn't see.

We truly started talking in the final quarter of 2025, and our friendship grew rapidly. By December that same year, we officially began our courtship. We got engaged on June 17, 2026, and now we are looking forward to tying the knot traditionally and in church this September 19th!

Thank you to our amazing family and friends who have supported us this far. Keep us in your prayers as we take this big step towards spending our lives together.

How to Get to Ile-Ife

✈️

By Air

Fly into Ibadan Airport or Osogbo Airport (Osun State). From either, hire a car or taxi to Ile-Ife (approx. 1–1.5 hours).

🚌

By Road from Lagos

Bus or hire a car from Lagos (approx. 3–4 hours via Ibadan-Ife expressway). ABC Transport and GUO Transport service this route regularly.

🚗

By Road from Ibadan

Ile-Ife is approximately 1 hour from Ibadan via the Ibadan-Ife expressway — a straightforward, well-travelled route.
